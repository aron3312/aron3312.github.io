哈梅恩的吹笛人

The Pied Piper of Hamelin

你是傳說中的吹笛人。在吹響笛子之前，擺好路障、音符和起司，讓每一隻老鼠都走進山洞，而不是掉進河裡或跑進城鎮。

You are the Pied Piper of the legend. Before you play the flute, place barricades, notes and cheese so that every rat walks into the cave instead of the river or the town.
