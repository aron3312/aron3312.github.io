魔笛鼠鼠回家囉

Hamelin Squeaks

拿起魔笛，把一群迷路的鼠鼠平安帶回山洞！吹笛之前擺好路障、音符和起司，別讓牠們掉進河裡或跑進城鎮。

Pick up the magic flute and lead a squeaky crowd home! Before you play, place barricades, notes and cheese so every little mouse scurries into the cave instead of the river or the town.

卡舖人生

Card Shop Life

在街角開一家球員卡店：批貨、賣卡包、在櫃檯殺價收卡、看穿假卡，一年內存到錢把店面買下來。

Open a sports card shop on the corner: stock boxes, sell packs, haggle at the counter and catch fakes, and save enough to buy the shop within a year.

小貼出發！

Sticky Steps

拍一張照片、剪下一塊，幫小貼鋪一條路！照片裡是什麼決定紙片能做什麼：紅色融冰、藍色滅火、黃色導電、綠色澆水會長大。

Snap a photo, cut out a piece and build a path for Sticky. What's in the photo decides what the piece can do: red melts ice, blue puts out fire, yellow conducts, green grows. (The game is in Traditional Chinese.)
