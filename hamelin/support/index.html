常見問題

我的進度存在哪裡？

進度只存在你的裝置上，沒有雲端備份。刪除本遊戲會一併刪除進度，重新安裝後需要從頭開始。

怎麼開啟 3 倍速？

點速度按鈕可在 1.5 倍、2 倍之間切換。3 倍速需要觀看一則簡短的獎勵廣告，每次進入關卡看一次即可。

為什麼遊戲顯示英文？

裝置語言設為中文時，遊戲會顯示繁體中文；其他語言則顯示英文。若想玩中文版，請在 iOS「設定」→「一般」→「語言與地區」中把中文加入偏好語言，或在「設定」→「Hamelin」（英文裝置上的 App 名稱）→「語言」中為本遊戲選擇中文。

FAQ

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device; there is no cloud backup. Deleting the game deletes your progress, and a reinstall starts from the beginning.

How do I get 3x speed?

Tap the speed button to switch between 1.5x and 2x. 3x speed needs one short rewarded ad, once each time you enter a level.

Why is the game in English?

The game shows Traditional Chinese when your device is set to Chinese and English otherwise. To play in Chinese, add Chinese to your preferred languages in iOS Settings → General → Language & Region, or choose Chinese for this game alone in Settings → Hamelin → Language.
