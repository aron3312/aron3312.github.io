隱私權政策

《魔笛鼠鼠回家囉》iOS 版 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日
本次更新：遊戲進度可以存在你自己的 iCloud；廣告同意可以在遊戲的「設定」裡更改（皆為 1.1 版起）。

本政策說明 iOS 遊戲《魔笛鼠鼠回家囉》（以下稱「本遊戲」，由個人開發者開發與發行，以下稱「我們」）如何處理你的資料。

帳號與資料存放

  • 本遊戲沒有帳號，也不需要登入。
  • 遊戲進度、設定與遊戲為你產生的暱稱儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳給我們。
  • 從 1.1 版起，如果你的 iPhone 登入了 iCloud，遊戲進度（各關星數、隱藏任務、解鎖的造型、圖鑑和造型選擇）也會在你自己的 iCloud 存一份，讓你換手機或重新安裝後拿回進度。這份資料由 Apple 保存在你的 iCloud 帳號裡，我們看不到，也無法取得。體力、設定和遊戲統計不會存到 iCloud。
  • 本遊戲沒有自己的伺服器。目前沒有線上多人連線；多人模式是在你的裝置上與電腦玩家對戰。為了改善遊戲，本遊戲會送出匿名的遊戲統計（見「遊戲統計」）。

廣告（Google AdMob）

本遊戲透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告（例如觀看獎勵廣告以使用 3 倍速）。為了顯示廣告並衡量成效，Google 可能會收集：

  • 裝置識別碼。只有在你允許「追蹤」時，才會使用 iOS 廣告識別碼（IDFA）。
  • 由 IP 位址推算的大略位置。
  • 使用與診斷資料，例如廣告的顯示與點擊、應用程式效能與當機資訊。

Google 如何使用這些資料，請參閱 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料。

我們將廣告內容限制在「G」級（適合所有年齡層）。

遊戲統計（Amplitude）

為了知道關卡是不是太難、玩家在哪裡卡關，本遊戲會把匿名的遊戲統計送到 Amplitude（美國的分析服務，代我們處理資料）：

  • 玩了哪些關卡、結果（過關或失敗、星數）、花了多久、放了哪些道具、選了哪種播放速度
  • 體力用完、看了獎勵廣告與否、對戰電腦的名次、是否打開造型和圖鑑
  • App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、語言，以及裝置設定的國家或地區

這些統計用的是本遊戲自己產生的隨機編號，不是你的姓名、帳號、Apple ID 或廣告識別碼（IDFA），也不會用來追蹤你或投放廣告。我們只看整體的數字。

位於歐洲經濟區（EEA）、英國或瑞士的玩家，本遊戲不會送出任何遊戲統計。

同意與追蹤

  • 位於歐洲經濟區（EEA）或英國的玩家，會先看到 Google 的同意表單，可選擇是否同意個人化廣告。若要更改選擇，請到遊戲的「設定」→「廣告同意設定」（1.1 版起；較早的版本請刪除後重新安裝本遊戲）。
  • iOS 會詢問你是否允許本遊戲追蹤。不允許也能正常遊戲，只是看到的廣告較不個人化。
  • 是否允許「追蹤」不影響遊戲統計：遊戲統計本來就不會追蹤你。

如何停用追蹤與個人化廣告

  • iOS「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「追蹤」：可關閉本遊戲或所有 App 的追蹤請求。
  • iOS「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「Apple 廣告」：可關閉 Apple 的個人化廣告。

兒童

本遊戲適合一般大眾，並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計。我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

資料刪除

刪除本遊戲會刪除裝置上的所有遊戲資料（包括進度）。如果你的進度也存在 iCloud（1.1 版起），iCloud 裡的那一份會保留，重新安裝後會回來；你可以在 iOS「設定」→ 你的名字 →「iCloud」關閉本遊戲使用 iCloud，或在那裡管理 iCloud 儲存空間。遊戲統計不含能認出你的資訊，所以我們無法找出或刪除某一位玩家的統計；刪除並重新安裝本遊戲後，會換成一個新的隨機編號。

聯絡我們

對本政策有任何問題，請寄信至 support@piperpuzzles.com。

政策變更

若本政策有所變更，我們會更新本頁內容與上方的生效日期。重大變更也會在遊戲更新說明中告知。

Privacy Policy

Hamelin Squeaks for iOS · Effective date: November 10, 2026
This update: your progress can be kept in your own iCloud, and ad consent can be changed in the game’s Settings (both from version 1.1).

This policy explains how the iOS game Hamelin Squeaks (“the game”, made and published by an independent developer, “we”) handles your data.

Accounts and where your data is kept

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your progress, settings and the nickname the game makes up for you are stored on your device and are never sent to us.
  • From version 1.1, if your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, your progress (stars on each level, hidden missions, unlocked outfits, the field guide and your chosen look) is also kept in your own iCloud, so it comes back on a new phone or after reinstalling. Apple keeps this copy in your iCloud account; we can’t see or access it. Hearts, settings and game statistics are not stored in iCloud.
  • The game has no server of its own. There is no online multiplayer at present: multiplayer matches are played against computer players on your device. To improve the game, it sends anonymous gameplay statistics (see “Game statistics”).

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (for example, a rewarded ad to unlock 3x speed). To show ads and measure them, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers. The iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking.
  • Coarse location derived from your IP address.
  • Usage and diagnostic data, such as ad impressions and clicks, app performance and crash information.

To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We limit ad content to the “G” rating (suitable for all audiences).

Game statistics (Amplitude)

To learn whether levels are too hard and where players get stuck, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Amplitude (an analytics service in the United States that processes them on our behalf):

  • Which levels you play, how they end (cleared or failed, stars), how long they take, which items you place and which playback speed you choose
  • Running out of hearts, whether you watch rewarded ads, your place in matches against the computer, and whether you open the wardrobe and the field guide
  • App version, device model, iOS version, language, and the country or region set on your device

These statistics use a random number the game creates, not your name, an account, your Apple ID or the advertising identifier (IDFA), and they are never used to track you or to target ads. We only look at overall numbers.

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland, the game sends no gameplay statistics.

Consent and tracking

  • Players in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK first see Google’s consent form, where they can choose whether to allow personalised ads. To change your choice later, go to the game’s Settings → Ad consent choices (from version 1.1; on earlier versions, delete and reinstall the game).
  • iOS asks whether you allow the game to track you. The game works the same either way; you just see less personalised ads.
  • Allowing or declining tracking doesn’t change the game statistics: they never track you either way.

How to opt out

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking: turn off tracking requests for this game or for all apps.
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising: turn off Apple’s personalised ads.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game deletes all of its data on your device, including your progress. If your progress is also kept in iCloud (from version 1.1), that copy stays and comes back when you reinstall; you can stop the game from using iCloud, or manage your iCloud storage, in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. Game statistics contain nothing that identifies you, so we can’t find or delete one player’s statistics; after you delete and reinstall the game, it uses a new random number.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@piperpuzzles.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game’s update notes.
