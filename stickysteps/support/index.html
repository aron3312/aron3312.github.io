常見問題

一定要拍照才能玩嗎？

不用。每一關都可以用遊戲內建的「剪貼簿」過關。拍照或從相簿挑照片是更好玩的玩法：身邊的東西是什麼顏色、多亮，就決定剪下來的紙片能做什麼。

照片會被上傳嗎？

不會。照片只在你的手機上處理，遊戲讀取你剪下那一塊的顏色和亮度，把它變成關卡裡的紙片。

我剪的紙片為什麼沒有用？

拿著紙片時，下方的屬性卡會顯示它的顏色和能力。顏色要佔紙片的一半以上才有能力（例如紅色才會融冰），很亮的才會飄、很暗的才會重。剪的時候也能先看「剪下來會是」的預覽。

卡關了怎麼辦？

按左上角的暫停，選「看提示」，看一則獎勵廣告就會顯示這一關的解法：要剪什麼紙、放在哪裡。每一關的提示都保證解得開，解鎖一次之後就一直可以看。

體力是什麼？

過一個新關卡會用掉 1 點體力，最多 5 點，每 10 分鐘回 1 點。失敗、重來和重玩已經過的關卡都不扣；第一章不用體力。體力不夠時可以看一則獎勵廣告換 1 點。

我的進度存在哪裡？

進度只存在你的裝置上，沒有雲端備份。刪除本遊戲會一併刪除進度。

遊戲有英文嗎？

目前遊戲只有繁體中文。

FAQ

Do I have to take photos?

No. Every level can be cleared with the game’s built-in scrapbook. Taking a photo or picking one from your album is the fun part: the colour and lightness of what you photograph decide what the cut piece can do.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos are processed on your phone only; the game reads the colour and lightness of the piece you cut and turns it into a piece in the level.

Why doesn’t my piece work?

While you hold a piece, the card at the bottom shows its colour and powers. A colour has to cover at least half of the piece to give it a power (red melts ice, for example); only very light pieces float and only very dark ones are heavy. You can also check the preview while cutting.

I’m stuck. What can I do?

Tap pause at the top left and choose the hint. After one rewarded ad, the level’s answer is shown: which paper to cut and where it goes. Every hint is guaranteed to work, and once unlocked it stays unlocked.

What is stamina?

Clearing a new level uses 1 stamina; you have up to 5, and 1 comes back every 10 minutes. Failing, retrying and replaying cleared levels are free, and so is the first chapter. When you run out, one rewarded ad gives 1 back.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device; there is no cloud backup. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

Is the game in English?

Not yet: the game’s text is in Traditional Chinese.
