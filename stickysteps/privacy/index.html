隱私權政策

《小貼出發！》iOS 版 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 13 日

本政策說明 iOS 遊戲《小貼出發！》（以下稱「本遊戲」，由個人開發者開發與發行，以下稱「我們」）如何處理你的資料。

帳號與資料存放

  • 本遊戲沒有帳號，也不需要登入。
  • 遊戲進度、體力、手帳的貼紙和解鎖的提示都儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳給我們。
  • 如果你的 iPhone 登入了 iCloud，遊戲進度（過了哪些關、解鎖的提示、看過的能力說明、手帳的貼紙和剩下的 2 倍速時間）也會在你自己的 iCloud 存一份，讓你換手機或重新安裝後拿回進度。這份資料由 Apple 保存在你的 iCloud 帳號裡，我們看不到，也無法取得。體力、設定和遊戲統計不會存到 iCloud。
  • 本遊戲沒有自己的伺服器。為了改善遊戲，本遊戲會送出匿名的遊戲統計（見「遊戲統計」）。

相機與照片

  • 只有在你選擇「拍照」或從「相簿」挑一張照片時，本遊戲才會使用相機或照片。不拍照也能玩：遊戲內建的「剪貼簿」可以過所有關卡。
  • 照片只在你的裝置上處理：遊戲讀取你剪下那一塊的顏色、亮度和紋理，把它變成關卡裡的紙片。照片不會上傳，也不會被遊戲另外存下來。
  • 過關後的分享圖是由你自己透過 iOS 的分享選單送出的，我們收不到。

廣告（Google AdMob）

本遊戲透過 Google AdMob 顯示獎勵廣告，只有在你自己選擇時才會播放（例如換一點體力、看提示或 2 倍速）。為了顯示廣告並衡量成效，Google 可能會收集：

  • 裝置識別碼。只有在你允許「追蹤」時，才會使用 iOS 廣告識別碼（IDFA）。
  • 由 IP 位址推算的大略位置。
  • 使用與診斷資料，例如廣告的顯示與點擊、應用程式效能與當機資訊。

Google 如何使用這些資料，請參閱 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料。

我們將廣告內容限制在「G」級（適合所有年齡層）。

遊戲統計（Amplitude）

為了知道關卡是不是太難、玩家在哪裡卡關，本遊戲會把匿名的遊戲統計送到 Amplitude（美國的分析服務，代我們處理資料）：

  • 玩了哪些關卡、結果（過關、失敗的原因）、用了幾張紙片、是否看了提示
  • 剪下的紙片的顏色和能力，以及照片來自相機或相簿、還是遊戲內建的剪貼簿（不包含照片本身）
  • 體力用完、是否看了獎勵廣告、每日手帳的簽到與挑戰、是否分享了過關圖
  • App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、語言，以及裝置設定的國家或地區

這些統計用的是隨機產生的編號，不是你的姓名、帳號、Apple ID 或廣告識別碼（IDFA），也不會用來追蹤你或投放廣告。我們只看整體的數字。

位於歐洲經濟區（EEA）、英國或瑞士的玩家，本遊戲不會送出任何遊戲統計。

同意與追蹤

  • 位於歐洲經濟區（EEA）、英國或瑞士的玩家，會先看到 Google 的同意表單，可選擇是否同意個人化廣告。若要更改選擇，請點遊戲主選單右上角的齒輪「設定」，再點「廣告隱私設定」。
  • iOS 會詢問你是否允許本遊戲追蹤。不允許也能正常遊戲，只是看到的廣告較不個人化。
  • 是否允許「追蹤」不影響遊戲統計：遊戲統計本來就不會追蹤你。

如何停用追蹤與個人化廣告

  • iOS「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「追蹤」：可關閉本遊戲或所有 App 的追蹤請求。
  • iOS「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「Apple 廣告」：可關閉 Apple 的個人化廣告。
  • iOS「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「相機」或「照片」：可隨時收回本遊戲使用相機或照片的權限。

兒童

本遊戲適合一般大眾，並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計。我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

資料刪除

刪除本遊戲會刪除裝置上的所有遊戲資料（包括進度與手帳）。存在你 iCloud 裡的那一份會保留，重新安裝後會回來；你可以在 iOS「設定」→ 你的名字 →「iCloud」關閉本遊戲使用 iCloud，或在那裡管理 iCloud 儲存空間。遊戲統計不含能認出你的資訊，所以我們無法找出或刪除某一位玩家的統計；刪除並重新安裝本遊戲後，會換成一個新的隨機編號。

聯絡我們

對本政策有任何問題，請寄信至 support@piperpuzzles.com。

政策變更

若本政策有所變更，我們會更新本頁內容與上方的生效日期。重大變更也會在遊戲更新說明中告知。

Privacy Policy

Sticky Steps for iOS · Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how the iOS game Sticky Steps (“the game”, made and published by an independent developer, “we”) handles your data.

Accounts and where your data is kept

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your progress, stamina, planner stickers and unlocked hints are stored on your device and are never sent to us.
  • If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, your progress (levels cleared, unlocked hints, power cards seen, planner stickers and any 2× speed left) is also kept in your own iCloud, so it comes back on a new phone or after reinstalling. Apple keeps this copy in your iCloud account; we can’t see or access it. Stamina, settings and game statistics are not stored in iCloud.
  • The game has no server of its own. To improve the game, it sends anonymous gameplay statistics (see “Game statistics”).

Camera and photos

  • The game uses the camera or your photos only when you choose to take a photo or pick one from your album. You can play without them: the built-in scrapbook can clear every level.
  • Photos are processed on your device only: the game reads the colour, lightness and texture of the piece you cut and turns it into a piece in the level. Photos are never uploaded, and the game doesn’t keep a copy of them.
  • The picture you can share after a win is sent by you, through the iOS share sheet; we don’t receive it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one (for example, for a point of stamina, a hint or 2x speed). To show ads and measure them, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers. The iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking.
  • Coarse location derived from your IP address.
  • Usage and diagnostic data, such as ad impressions and clicks, app performance and crash information.

To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We limit ad content to the “G” rating (suitable for all audiences).

Game statistics (Amplitude)

To learn whether levels are too hard and where players get stuck, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Amplitude (an analytics service in the United States that processes them on our behalf):

  • Which levels you play, how they end (cleared, or why they failed), how many pieces you used and whether you used a hint
  • The colour and powers of the pieces you cut, and whether the photo came from the camera or album or the built-in scrapbook (never the photo itself)
  • Running out of stamina, whether you watch rewarded ads, the daily planner’s check-ins and challenges, and whether you share a win
  • App version, device model, iOS version, language, and the country or region set on your device

These statistics use a randomly generated number, not your name, an account, your Apple ID or the advertising identifier (IDFA), and they are never used to track you or to target ads. We only look at overall numbers.

For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland, the game sends no gameplay statistics.

Consent and tracking

  • Players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland first see Google’s consent form, where they can choose whether to allow personalised ads. To change your choice later, open Settings (the gear at the top right of the game’s main menu) and tap Ad privacy settings.
  • iOS asks whether you allow the game to track you. The game works the same either way; you just see less personalised ads.
  • Allowing or declining tracking doesn’t change the game statistics: they never track you either way.

How to opt out

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking: turn off tracking requests for this game or for all apps.
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising: turn off Apple’s personalised ads.
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera or Photos: take back the game’s access to the camera or your photos at any time.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game deletes all of its data on your device, including your progress and planner. The copy in your iCloud stays and comes back when you reinstall; you can stop the game from using iCloud, or manage your iCloud storage, in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud. Game statistics contain nothing that identifies you, so we can’t find or delete one player’s statistics; after you delete and reinstall the game, it uses a new random number.

Questions about this policy: support@piperpuzzles.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game’s update notes.
