常見問題

我的進度存在哪裡？

存檔只存在你的裝置上，沒有雲端備份，遊戲會自動存檔。刪除本遊戲會一併刪除存檔、成就和卡片圖鑑，重新安裝後需要從頭開始。

店倒了，之前的東西都不見了嗎？

那一局會結束，但成就、紀錄和卡片圖鑑會保留下來，下一局繼續累積。

遊戲裡有廣告嗎？

只有你自己選擇要不要看的獎勵廣告，從第 2 個月開始出現：月底讓下個月房租打九折、早上免費拆一包、出門找貨時再來一次。不看也能玩完一整年，沒有插播廣告，也沒有 App 內購買。

為什麼遊戲顯示英文？

裝置語言設為中文時，遊戲會顯示繁體中文；其他語言則顯示英文。若想玩中文版，請在 iOS「設定」→「一般」→「語言與地區」中把中文加入偏好語言，或在「設定」→「Card Shop」（英文裝置上的 App 名稱）→「語言」中為本遊戲選擇中文。

怎麼關掉遊戲統計？

進到店裡後，點左上角的齒輪打開「設定」，把「分享遊玩數據」關掉即可。

FAQ

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device, and the game saves as you play; there is no cloud backup. Deleting the game deletes your saved game, achievements and card album, and a reinstall starts from the beginning.

My shop closed. Is everything gone?

That run is over, but your achievements, records and card album stay, and keep growing in your next run.

Are there ads?

Only rewarded ads you choose to watch, from the second month: 10% off next month’s rent at month end, a free pack in the morning, and one more go when you’re out for stock. You can play the whole year without one. There are no interstitial ads and no in-app purchases.

Why is the game in English?

The game shows Traditional Chinese when your device is set to Chinese and English otherwise. To play in Chinese, add Chinese to your preferred languages in iOS Settings → General → Language & Region, or choose Chinese for this game alone in Settings → Card Shop → Language.

How do I turn off game statistics?

Once you are in your shop, tap the gear at the top left to open Settings and turn off “Share play stats”.
