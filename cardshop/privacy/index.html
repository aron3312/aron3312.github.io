隱私權政策

《卡舖人生》iOS 版 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 11 日

本政策說明 iOS 遊戲《卡舖人生》（以下稱「本遊戲」，由個人開發者開發與發行，以下稱「我們」）如何處理你的資料。

帳號與資料存放

  • 本遊戲沒有帳號，也不需要登入。
  • 存檔、設定、成就和卡片圖鑑都儲存在你的裝置上，不會傳給我們。
  • 本遊戲沒有自己的伺服器，也沒有線上連線的功能。為了改善遊戲，本遊戲會送出匿名的遊戲統計（見「遊戲統計」）。
  • 本遊戲沒有 App 內購買。卡包只能用遊戲裡賺的錢買。

廣告（Google AdMob）

本遊戲透過 Google AdMob 顯示獎勵廣告，只有你自己選擇觀看時才會播放（例如月底看廣告讓下個月房租打九折）。為了顯示廣告並衡量成效，Google 可能會收集：

  • 裝置識別碼。只有在你允許「追蹤」時，才會使用 iOS 廣告識別碼（IDFA）。
  • 由 IP 位址推算的大略位置。
  • 使用與診斷資料，例如廣告的顯示與點擊、應用程式效能與當機資訊。

Google 如何使用這些資料，請參閱 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料。

我們將廣告內容限制在「G」級（適合所有年齡層）。

遊戲統計（Amplitude）

為了知道遊戲是不是太難、玩家在哪裡放棄，本遊戲會把匿名的遊戲統計送到 Amplitude（美國的分析服務，代我們處理資料）：

  • 開啟遊戲、開新店時選的身分、教學有沒有看完
  • 每個月底的結果（現金、資產、聲譽、目標有沒有達成、出門找貨的次數）、押金有沒有付出來、一局怎麼結束
  • 打開了哪些畫面（例如圖鑑、成就、設定），以及有沒有看完獎勵廣告
  • App 版本、裝置型號、iOS 版本、語言，以及裝置設定的國家或地區

這些統計用的是分析工具自己產生的隨機編號，不是你的姓名、帳號、Apple ID 或廣告識別碼（IDFA），也不會用來追蹤你或投放廣告。我們只看整體的數字。

你可以隨時在遊戲的「設定」關閉「分享遊玩數據」，之後就不會再送出。位於歐洲經濟區（EEA）、英國或瑞士的玩家，本遊戲不會送出任何遊戲統計。

同意與追蹤

  • 第一次出現廣告時（遊戲的第 2 個月），位於歐洲經濟區（EEA）、英國或瑞士的玩家會先看到 Google 的同意表單，可選擇是否同意個人化廣告。之後要更改，請到遊戲的「設定」→「廣告同意設定」。
  • 接著 iOS 會詢問你是否允許本遊戲追蹤。不允許也能正常遊戲，只是看到的廣告較不個人化。
  • 是否允許「追蹤」不影響遊戲統計：遊戲統計本來就不會追蹤你。

如何停用追蹤與個人化廣告

  • iOS「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「追蹤」：可關閉本遊戲或所有 App 的追蹤請求。
  • iOS「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「Apple 廣告」：可關閉 Apple 的個人化廣告。

兒童

本遊戲適合一般大眾，並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計。我們不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

資料刪除

刪除本遊戲會刪除裝置上的所有遊戲資料，包括存檔、成就和圖鑑。遊戲統計不含能認出你的資訊，所以我們無法找出或刪除某一位玩家的統計；刪除並重新安裝本遊戲後，會換成一個新的隨機編號。

聯絡我們

對本政策有任何問題，請寄信至 support@piperpuzzles.com。

政策變更

若本政策有所變更，我們會更新本頁內容與上方的生效日期。重大變更也會在遊戲更新說明中告知。

Privacy Policy

Card Shop Life for iOS · Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how the iOS game Card Shop Life (“the game”, made and published by an independent developer, “we”) handles your data.

Accounts and where your data is kept

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your saved game, settings, achievements and card album are stored on your device and are never sent to us.
  • The game has no server of its own and nothing online to play. To improve the game, it sends anonymous gameplay statistics (see “Game statistics”).
  • The game has no in-app purchases. Card packs are bought only with money earned in the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one (for example, an ad at month end for 10% off next month’s rent). To show ads and measure them, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers. The iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking.
  • Coarse location derived from your IP address.
  • Usage and diagnostic data, such as ad impressions and clicks, app performance and crash information.

To learn how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We limit ad content to the “G” rating (suitable for all audiences).

Game statistics (Amplitude)

To learn whether the game is too hard and where players give up, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics to Amplitude (an analytics service in the United States that processes them on our behalf):

  • Opening the game, which start you pick for a new shop, and whether you finish the tutorial
  • Each month’s results (cash, net worth, reputation, whether the month’s goal was met, how often you went out for stock), whether the deposit was paid, and how a run ends
  • Which screens you open (such as the album, achievements and settings), and whether you watch rewarded ads to the end
  • App version, device model, iOS version, language, and the country or region set on your device

These statistics use a random number the analytics tool creates, not your name, an account, your Apple ID or the advertising identifier (IDFA), and they are never used to track you or to target ads. We only look at overall numbers.

You can turn off “Share play stats” in the game’s Settings at any time, and nothing more is sent. For players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland, the game sends no gameplay statistics.

Consent and tracking

  • The first time an ad is offered (in the game’s second month), players in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland first see Google’s consent form, where they can choose whether to allow personalised ads. To change your choice later, go to the game’s Settings → Ad consent choices.
  • Then iOS asks whether you allow the game to track you. The game works the same either way; you just see less personalised ads.
  • Allowing or declining tracking doesn’t change the game statistics: they never track you either way.

How to opt out

  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking: turn off tracking requests for this game or for all apps.
  • iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising: turn off Apple’s personalised ads.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the game deletes all of its data on your device, including your saved game, achievements and album. Game statistics contain nothing that identifies you, so we can’t find or delete one player’s statistics; after you delete and reinstall the game, it uses a new random number.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@piperpuzzles.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the game’s update notes.
